Community Guidelines

Use CSHub responsibly

1

Keep discussions respectful

Ask questions clearly, reply constructively, and avoid personal attacks, harassment, or dismissive comments.

2

Use Study Buddy for real study plans

Create sessions with accurate unit codes, topics, times, locations, capacity, and mode so other students can plan confidently.

3

Do not share private information

Keep passwords, student IDs, private contact details, and sensitive course information out of public messages.

4

Protect academic integrity

Use sessions for revision, explanation, planning, and collaboration. Do not request or provide answers intended for submission.

5

Keep announcements trustworthy

Announcements are for official platform and course notices. Admin users should write clear summaries and complete details.

6

Leave sessions you no longer need

If you cannot attend or no longer need a group, leave it so another student can take the available place.
